# Supermarket-sales-dashboard-powerbi

Sales Performance Dashboard 📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing supermarket sales performance across branches, cities, product lines, and customer segments.

Show Image

Overview

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, helping stakeholders quickly identify trends in revenue, customer behavior, product performance, and payment preferences across three branches (A, B, C) and three cities (Naypyitaw, Yangon, Mandalay).

Key Features
KPI Summary Cards — Total Sales, Gross Income, Quantity Sold, Average Rating, and Total Transactions at a glance
Interactive Filters — Slice data by Date, Branch, Month, Payment Method, and Quantity
Sales Breakdown — By branch, city, product line, gender, and customer type
Trend Analysis — Monthly sales trend and monthly sales by branch
Payment Insights — Waterfall chart showing sales contribution by payment method (Cash, Ewallet, Credit Card)
Product Performance — Gross income by product line and average customer rating by product line

Dataset

The dashboard is built on a supermarket sales dataset containing the following fields:

Branch, City, Customer type, Date, Gender, Gross income, Gross margin, Invoice ID, Product line, Quantity, Rating, Tax 5%, Time, Total, Unit price, Payment

How to Use
Clone or download this repository
Open sales_dashboard.pbix in Power BI Desktop
Use the filter panel on the left to explore sales by Branch, Month, Payment, or Quantity
Hover over charts for detailed tooltips
Key Insights
Branch C leads in total sales (108K), followed by Branch A (104K) and Branch B (102K)
Naypyitaw is the top-performing city, closely followed by Yangon and Mandalay
Food and beverages is the top-performing product line by gross income
Credit card is the most-used payment method, followed by Ewallet and Cash
Sales are nearly evenly split between Member and Normal customers (49% vs 51%)
